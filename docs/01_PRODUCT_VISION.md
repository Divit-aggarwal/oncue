# Product Vision

> Status: draft assembled from `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. Awaiting creator review; the creator owns this document.

## Purpose

Make the creator faster at producing technically accurate, entertaining, recognizable animated explainers for an Indian audience, in Hinglish and English, narrated in the creator's own voice.

## Principles

* The creator is the director and final authority. The system handles complexity and repetition.
* Human approval at the creative gate and the production gate.
* The creator's recorded delivery drives the timeline.
* Local-first, inexpensive to operate, and reproducible.
* Technical correctness comes before comedy. Analogies may simplify but must not misrepresent the concept.
* Consistency through reuse of approved components once the creator's visual universe exists.

## Formats

* Short-form vertical video (Reels/Shorts). Implemented default: 1080×1920.
* Longer technical videos. Same pipeline, different configuration.

## Non-Goals

* Autonomously inventing the visual identity, characters, humor or brand.
* Cloud dependencies or recurring costs without explicit approval.
